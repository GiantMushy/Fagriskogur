Ancient History

A Rich Heritage of Family Retreat

Rebuilt from ground-up in 2022, the original Fagriskogur was built in 1942 and the site has been a cherished family retreat for generations.

Enveloped by the lush forest planted by the family over half a century ago, the lodge exudes a sense of timeless tranquility and natural beauty. Here, amidst the rustling leaves and gentle whispers of the wind, you’ll find a haven to relax, recharge, and reconnect with your loved ones.

Unique emotions with genuine hospitality

At Fagriskogur Lodge, your comfort and satisfaction are our top priorities. Our dedicated staff, including skilled chefs and attentive waiters, are on hand to ensure that every moment of your stay is nothing short of perfection. From the tantalizing aromas of freshly brewed coffee and full breakfast each morning to the exquisite flavors of gourmet dinners prepared with locally sourced ingredients, every culinary experience is a celebration of Icelandic hospitality and gastronomy.

Step inside and discover the unparalleled comfort and elegance of Fagriskogur Lodge. The spacious living area and a crackling fireplace, offers the perfect setting for cozy evenings with family and friends.

Golden Circle

Ideally situated on the famed Golden Circle route, Fagriskogur Lodge offers easy access to some of Iceland’s most iconic attractions.

From the majestic Gullfoss waterfall to the bubbling geothermal wonders of Geysir and the historic grounds of Thingvellir, every moment promises to be a journey of discovery and wonder. Nearby, the charming town of Selfoss beckons with its quaint shops and cultural delights, while the secret lagoon of Fludir offers a secluded oasis of relaxation.   How about an ice cave exploration or lunch in Reykholt while learning about farm life in Iceland at Fridheimar, where they harness geothermal energy and use artificial lights to grow tomatoes all year round.
